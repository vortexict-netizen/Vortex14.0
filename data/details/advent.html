GENERAL SERIES

Advent

Advertising competition across solo and team categories


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Format: Advent will be held in two categories:

  • Team participation (3-4 members per team)
  • Solo Participation

Key Rules:

  • The Advent team will judge rounds 1 and 3.
  • Round 2 will be judged by a Panel of judges.
  • Submissions for Round 1 via vortexadvent@gmail.com.
  • Rounds 2 and 3 are conducted offline during Vortex week (Dates TBA).

Rules & Rounds

Round 1: Screening

Solo & Team: Participants choose between poster, jingle, reel, or animation video.

  • Poster: Create your own product/company with brand name, logo, and tagline. Judged on branding strategy and originality.
  • Jingle/Reel/Animation: Existing brand names allowed. Judged on originality and storytelling creativity. Non-participant individuals can be featured.
  • Submission: Via Google Form (Deadline TBA).

Round 2: Advert Presentation

Solo Participants: Option to form a team with other solos or join existing 3-member teams.

  • Team: Category announced a day prior. Prepare impromptu skit (max 1.5 min) or jingle. Props allowed.
  • Judged on Creativity, Execution, Ingenuity, and Overall Impression.
  • Selected teams advance to Round 3 based on Round 2 scores.

Round 3: Quiz

Format: 3 sub-rounds, 2 questions per team each:

  • Identifying logos
  • Identifying ads
  • Identifying taglines

Scoring:

  • Direct correct: +10 points
  • Direct incorrect: -10 points
  • Passed to next team: No penalty
  • First pass attempt: 5 points | Second: 3 points | Third: 2 points
  • Incorrect pass attempt: Negative equal to attempted value

Winner: Highest cumulative score from Rounds 2 and 3.
