PHARMA SERIES

Blueprint

Design a production facility and present solutions to real industry problems


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Blueprint is one of the important events of the Pharma series in VORTEX. Participants design a production facility based on a problem and present it to a panel of judges.

Key Features:

  • Problem statements provided directly by industry leaders about actual issues faced by the industry.
  • Use technical and practical knowledge for innovative and effective solutions.
  • Solutions analyzed by industry experts.
  • Tests entrepreneurial skills and compliance with legal and environmental parameters.

Participation: Individual or group of two or three from any college.

Note: The judging panel reserves the right to disqualify a team if any rules are breached. Problem statements will be released 10 days prior to the event day.

Rules & Rounds

Round 1: Offline Campus Challenge

This round would be offline in the ICT Mumbai campus.

  • Individuals/teams provided with question statement related to pharmaceutical industrial equipment, processes and innovation.
  • On solving the question, a simple task with a clue to be done within campus of ICT Mumbai.
  • On completion of task, individual/team returns to main site to solve next question and task.
  • Each question and task round: 20 minutes with total of three tasks. No extra time provided.
  • First 10 teams to answer questions correctly and complete all tasks correctly in least time selected for next round.
  • No negative marking for this round.

Round 2: Abstract Submission

Problem statements will be released and participants expected to send a typed PDF as an abstract explaining their solution.

Selection: 5 teams selected as finalists.

Submission:

  • Solutions must be sent within time limit; no solutions accepted beyond deadline (TBA).
  • All entries sent to blueprint.vortex13.0@gmail.com
  • Selection based on problem-solving skills and feasibility of solution.
  • Decision made by judges is final.
  • Selected participants informed via mail or personal contact.

Details to Include at Start:

  • Names of the participants
  • Email ID of any one participant
  • Vortex Registration ID of the members
  • Contact number of both participants

Format of Abstract:

  • PDF format only
  • Minimum 2 pages, Maximum 5 pages
  • Font: Times New Roman, Size: 14
  • Title of solution report and team number clearly specified as given by event heads
  • Document named as assigned team number, e.g., 'Team 1_abstract'
  • Problem statement stated at start before writing solution
  • Direct solutions from internet will NOT be entertained and will lead to DISQUALIFICATION

Round 3: Final Presentation

Participants present their solutions in PowerPoint Presentations to a panel of judges.

  • Final round held in offline mode at ICT, Mumbai. Only for genuine reasons, participants allowed to give presentation online.
  • Guidelines for presentations will be given.
  • Mandatory to make presentation in given template.
  • Participants should send presentations one day prior to final round (Date TBA).
  • Finalists will be awarded based on judges' decision. Total 3 winners will be announced.

PowerPoint Presentation Rules:

  • Template for PowerPoint will be emailed to selected teams.
  • Should not exceed 10 slides, including title slide and thank-you slide.
  • Duration should not exceed 10 minutes.
  • No groups allowed to exceed time limit, solution should be explained properly.
  • There may be follow-up questions by judges; participants expected to be ready.
  • Presentation can include tables and pictures. Participants expected to explain tables if any.
  • All team members expected to take part in presentation and explain solution verbally.
