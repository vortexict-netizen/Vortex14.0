Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Welcome to ChemFlow! Dive into an electrifying challenge where knowledge meets strategy, and every decision counts. This isn't just a test of your technical skills; it's a battle of wits, economic savvy and Chemical engineering mastery.

Key Skills:

  • Chemical Engineering knowledge
  • Strategic thinking
  • Economic decision-making
  • Process design
  • Equipment selection

Strategise, outbid, and outperform as you navigate a journey that demands both brilliance and boldness. Gear up for the thrill- ChemFlow awaits!

Rules & Rounds

Round 1: Quiz Round

This round requires a basic understanding of chemical principles and their applications.

  • Duration: TBA (approx. 1 hour)
  • Format: Flexible format (can include MCQs, numerical questions, diagram problems, etc.)
  • Number of teams selected will be decided later.

Round 2: Rapid Fire Round

All selected teams will participate in a rapid fire challenge where quick thinking and accuracy are key.

  • Structure: Questions will be Objective MCQ type and displayed on the screen. No negative marking for MCQs. The round will occur in two slots, with half of the advancing teams competing in each slot. Each team will be given 3-5 seconds to provide the answer to the question.
  • Bonus Questions: Certain bonus questions will require calculation. These questions will double the weightage of MCQs and will have negative marking.
  • Currency System: Each question carries certain weightage or currency. Each right answer gives certain currency to the team. Currency earned is used in the next round. There will be a cut-off limit for the amount of currency. Teams unable to reach the limit will be eliminated.
  • Tie-Breaker - Sudden Death: If two teams end up with the same amount of currency, the tied teams play a 5-question rapid fire. The team with the most points advances to the final round.

Round 3: Final Auction Round

The ultimate test of process design and strategic equipment selection.

  • Problem Statement: Industrial process problem statement given before round start. The same problem statement applies to all teams. It provides raw materials required for the process and the desired end product. Teams design a flowsheet according to chemical engineering knowledge.
  • Equipment Auction: Necessary equipment required for the process will be auctioned. Each team can bid and buy equipment based on currency earned in the preceding round. Not every equipment displayed will be essentially useful; teams must match alertness with patience to select viable equipment.
  • Winning Criteria: The motive is to see how efficiently you make the process. The team whose process is most energy and economically efficient wins. The process should match the model answer as close as possible.
