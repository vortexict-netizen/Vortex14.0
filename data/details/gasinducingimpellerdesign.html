PRODIGY SERIES

Gas Inducing Impeller Design

Design and fabricate a gas-inducing impeller optimized for mass transfer and suspension at minimal speed and power.


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹10,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Team Event: Maximum 3 participants per team. Entry fee: ₹75 per participant. Maximum 10 teams.

Venue: Tinkerers Lab, ICT Mumbai

Objective: Design and fabricate a gas-inducing impeller that produces the highest mass transfer coefficient and off-bottom suspension at the lowest angular speed with the lowest power consumed.

Process Specs: Tank Volume: 500 ml | Liquid: Water | Gas: Air

Dimensional Constraints: Max impeller diameter: 8 cm | Minimum bottom clearance: 2 cm | Max shaft diameter: 10 mm

Fabrication Modes: 3D printing, Laser Cutting, and use of Power tools allowed. Welding is not possible.

Materials Available: Filaments (PLA, ABS, TPU), Acrylic sheets

Event Duration: 2-day event

Rules & Rounds

Problem Statement & Objective

Design and fabricate a gas-inducing impeller that maximizes mass transfer and achieves off-bottom suspension at the lowest possible angular speed with minimal power consumption. Process conditions: 500 ml water in tank, air as induced gas.

Constraints & Specifications

  • Maximum impeller diameter: 8 cm
  • Minimum clearance from reactor bottom: 2 cm
  • Maximum shaft diameter: 10 mm
  • Strict adherence required; deviation leads to failure.

Fabrication Guidelines

  • Allowed: 3D printing, Laser Cutting, and use of Power tools.
  • Not allowed: Welding.
  • Materials provided: PLA/ABS/TPU filaments and acrylic sheets.

Event Schedule

  • Day 1: 1st half: 3D modelling + sessions on 3D printing and laser cutting | 2nd half: Design and fabrication.
  • Day 2: 1st half: Design and fabrication | 2nd half: Testing and marking.

Evaluation & Result Analysis

The impeller must conform to dimensional criteria; non-compliance leads to failure.

Metrics:

  • Critical speed for gas induction
  • Off-bottom suspension speed
  • Rate to reach oxygen saturation
  • Power consumed (lower is better)
