Ignite

The flagship presentation event of Vortex 14.0


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Format: Ignite is a multi-round presentation competition featuring:

  • 20 slides presentation.
  • Each slide auto-advances after 15 seconds (5 minutes total).
  • Topics can be anything - technical, non-technical, humorous, historical, pop-culture or current affairs.
  • Presentation must be made on the provided template.

Judging:

  • Rounds 1-3: Judged by Event Heads.
  • Grand Finale: Combined scoring system (Judges' Scores + Audience Votes).
  • Audience votes hold weightage in deciding the final winner.

Rules & Rounds

Round I: Filtration

The first round tests creativity and timing through a Google form.

  • Selection Criteria: IMAGINATION
  • Most imaginative responses move forward to Round II.
  • Responses favored on a first come first serve basis.
  • Quick submission after form release is important.
  • Put your imagination to use and let the ingenuity flow!

Round II: Screening

Filtered candidates design PPTs on the provided IGNITE Template.

  • Choose ANY idea that comes to your mind.
  • Judged on creativity, proactiveness, and content.
  • Selection Criteria: CREATIVITY + PROACTIVENESS + CONTENT
  • Proactiveness to complete the PPT supplements your creativity.

Round III: Elimination

Screened candidates present their PPTs in front of judges.

  • Judged on oration, enthusiasm, and presentation skills.
  • Selection Criteria: ORATION + PRESENTATION + WITTINESS
  • Showcase your presentation skills and enlighten us with your ideas.

Round IV: Grand Finale

Welcome to the ENDGAME - the Crown Jewel of Vortex 14.0!

  • Finalists present in front of respected judges.
  • Selection Criteria: INGENUITY + RHETORICS + STAGE PRESENCE + RESILIENCE
  • Finalists will tackle a SURPRISE TASK given on the final day.
  • Grand, illustrious prize awaits the victors!

Presentation Rules:

  • Total 22 slides: 1 intro + 20 content + 1 time-up slide.
  • Cannot exceed 5 minutes total.
  • Slides cannot be skipped, paused, or shuffled during the presentation.
  • Topic can be anything but must not be controversial or hurtful.
  • No foul or NSFW language/content - leads to immediate disqualification.
  • Must use the provided template; effects should not tamper with slide timing.
