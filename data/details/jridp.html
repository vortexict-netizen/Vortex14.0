Jr. IDP

Junior Industry Defined Problems for first and second-year undergraduates


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹50,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Jr. IDP (Junior Industry Defined Problems) is Vortex's flagship event for first and second-year undergraduates. Ready to tackle real-world challenges in the chemical industries? This is your launchpad to post-ICT success!

Two Tailored Challenges:

  • First-year UGs: Dive into an accessible problem statement designed for beginners.
  • Second-year UGs: Tackle a more advanced challenge to sharpen your skills.

Team Format: Submit team solutions (max 3 members, any branch, any college) that shine in economy, practicality, and creativity. Individuals and teams of two are also permitted.

Why Join Jr. IDP?

  • Gain hands-on experience with chemical sector issues.
  • Showcase innovation to judges from the industry.
  • Win merit certificates and cash prizes.

Participation: Open to 1st and 2nd-year undergraduate students from all branches of study.

Rules & Rounds

Round 1: Abstract Submission

Teams are expected to submit a concise abstract detailing their proposed solution for the given problem statement.

  • The abstract should incorporate responses to posed questions.
  • Submission deadlines will be conveyed by Event Heads (TBA).
  • Evaluation Criteria: Innovation, thought process, and relevance to problem statement. Emphasis on highlighting the uniqueness of the solution.
  • The abstract serves as the primary determinant for progression to the next round.

Submission Requirements:

  • Email to: v.jridp@gmail.com
  • Subject line format: Jr. IDP PROBLEM STATEMENT DESIGNATION TEAM-name (e.g., Jr. IDP_FYUG_catalysts)
  • File name: TEAM name_abstract
  • Max 1-page PDF

Round 2: PPT Presentation

Teams selected from Round 1 prepare their solution's report and presentation.

Report Requirements:

  • Should include calculations supporting the proposed solution.
  • File name: TEAM-name_report
  • Max 15-page PDF

Presentation Requirements:

  • File name: TEAM-name_ppt
  • Max 15 slide pptx or pdf
  • Teams will present their solution before a seasoned judge.
  • Exact date and time will be conveyed by Event Heads (TBA).

Presentation Norms:

  • Should not exceed 20 minutes duration.
  • Warning bells will be rung before the last 2 minutes of presentation.
  • Teams with at least 1 member from ICT Mumbai Campus will have to present offline. Only then is hybrid mode permissible.
  • Presentations received via email before the deadline will be displayed from the event's laptop; teams will just have to present.

Recognition:

  • Top-performing teams from each year, as determined by the judge, receive merit certificates and prize money.
  • Awards are given during the prize distribution ceremony.

Submission:

  • Email to: v.jridp@gmail.com
  • Subject: Jr. IDP PROBLEM STATEMENT DESIGNATION TEAM-name
  • Attach report and presentation files.

Event Conduct Norms

  • A team can consist of a maximum of 3 participants. Individuals and teams of two are also permitted.
  • Participants within a team are not required to be from the same college.
  • Participation is open to 1st and 2nd-year undergraduate students from all branches of study.
  • All communications with participant team leaders will happen in a dedicated WhatsApp group.
  • Decisions of the judge and event heads are final and binding in all circumstances.
  • Participants can address any problems to event heads, who will make best efforts to resolve them.
  • Accurate data and references are mandatory for all submissions. All references must adhere to recognized formats followed by international journals.
  • Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. Any team found in violation will be immediately disqualified.
