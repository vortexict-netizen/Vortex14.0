  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Team Formation:

  • Teams can comprise individuals or groups of 2-3 members.
  • Each individual is permitted only one entry to maintain fairness and focus.
  • Exclusive problem statements provided by partnering NGOs.
  • Teams receive distinct problem statements, evenly distributed among participants.

Important Note: The judging panel reserves the rights to disqualify the participant team if any rules are breached. It is the responsibility of participants to keep themselves updated.

Rules & Rounds

Round 1: Social Impact Challenge Quiz (15 MCQs)

A quiz that tests awareness of real social issues + engineering logic, not just chemical theory.

Quiz Components:

  • Community Reality Quiz: Based on everyday environmental problems.
  • What Would You Fix? Scenario Cards: Each team gets a societal mini-scenario. Choose the most practical MCQ-solution option.
  • Ethical Dilemma MCQs: All options will be good — you have to pick the most ethical choice.

Round 2: Report Submission

Submission of at max three-page report/solution for the assigned problem statement.

Solutions must elaborate on:

  • Logic and practicality
  • Resource utilization and optimization
  • Sustainability and long-term impact

Report Format:

  • PDF of 2-3 pages
  • Font: Times New Roman, Size: 12
  • Title: Problem statement number and title (Font size: 14)
  • Document named the same way
  • Problem statement must be stated at the start, before the solution

Round 3: Presentation and Survey

Presentation:

  • Shortlisted teams create a concise at maximum 20-slide PowerPoint presentation.
  • Presentations limited to 10 minutes maximum.
  • Detail solution insights and analysis.

Survey Report:

  • Simultaneous submission of comprehensive survey report based on field visits and online research.
  • PDF of 1-2 pages
  • Font: Times New Roman, Size: 12
  • Title: Problem statement number and title (Font size: 14)

PPT Guidelines - Content Structure:

  • Clear, logical organization with seamless flow of ideas.
  • Comprehensive coverage of solution's components and rationale.

Visual Enhancements:

  • Creative and impactful use of visuals.
  • Engaging design elements enhancing presentation impact.

Delivery and Communication:

  • Engaging and confident delivery captivating audience attention.
  • Clarity and effectiveness in communicating solution details within time limit.

Survey Summary Guidelines:

  • Comprehensive summary of insights from field visits and online research.
  • Integration of survey findings into proposed solution strategy.
  • Effective utilization of diverse resources.
  • Clear and well-structured presentation of survey methodologies and outcomes.
  • Integration of community perspectives and feedback.
  • Adherence to ethical standards in conducting surveys and presenting findings.
