PRODIGY SERIES

Sr. IDP

Industry-Defined Problem - The flagship event with real industry challenges


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Fees

  • Prize Pool: ₹50,000 (per statement)
  • Registration Fee: ₹800 (for one problem statement)

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Industry-defined problem (IDP) is one of the most anticipated events of Vortex- The Chem-fest of Institute of Chemical Technology (formerly known as UDCT). This event requires participants to come up with innovative and multidisciplinary solutions for industry problems provided by the leaders of the industry.

Key Features: Participants are exposed to pressing issues in the industry. This gives an excellent opportunity to broaden horizons, apply learned knowledge and find innovative solutions through collaborations.

Eligibility: 3rd and 4th year undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate students with their respective college IDs are eligible.

Team Format: Only teams allowed. Team should consist of up to 3 members all of whom have registered in Vortex. Team members can be of same or different institution, same or different courses (UG, PG, PHD). All members required to have college IDs.

Rules & Rounds

Registration and Problem Statement Allotment

  • The registration form should be filled out along with payment before filling out the preference form.
  • Teams will fill out a preference form mentioning their preference for problem statements. In case the preference form is filled multiple times, the first entry will be considered.
  • Teams will be allotted problem statements on a first-come-first-serve basis (based on time and date of payment).
  • A PDF document of allotments will be shared to the email ID of the team leader.
  • A team ID would be allotted to every team after registration. A team can apply for 2 problem statements at max.

Multiple Problem Statements

  • Sr. IDP has allowed submission of more than one problem statement.
  • Registration fee for one problem: Rs. 800. For two or three problem statements: Rs. 1600.
  • The team has to fill out only one form on the Vortex website to register for multiple problem statements. Only a single preference form is given for a team to fill in preferences.
  • For teams registered for more than one problem statement, allotment is done on a first-come-first-serve basis. Late applications will not be entertained for multiple problem statements.
  • Presentations for multiple problem statements are held on the same day. Slots are arranged such that they do not overlap.

Abstract, Summary, and Presentation Requirements

  • Abstract Submission: Submit abstract before the deadline (TBA). The abstract will contain your understanding of the problem statement and should be within 200-400 words.
  • PowerPoint Presentation: Each team is required to prepare a PowerPoint Presentation explaining the solution to the IDP. Participants are given 10 minutes to present before judges, followed by a 5-minute Q&A session from judges. The audience will not be allowed to ask questions.
  • Project Report: Supporting the PowerPoint Presentation, participants are required to submit a detailed project report. It should contain all references, assumptions, and detailed solutions to the IDP including calculations and appendices.
  • Executive Summary: A 2-page executive summary of the solution is required for judges (Font: Times New Roman, Size: 12, Double spaced).
  • Submission: Participants must email a copy of the PPT, project report, and executive report to vortex.sridp@gmail.com with the subject: "Team ID- IDP(alphabet allotted)". No changes will be allowed after submission of documents. For Non-ICT students, online PPT presentation is also accepted.

Presentation Rules and Results

  • Not adhering to time constraints will result in negative marking.
  • To maintain transparency and unbiasedness, candidates are requested to refrain from using their institution name or logo. Even during the presentation, participants are requested to adhere to this rule, unless asked by judges. Failure to abide by this rule may lead to disqualification.
  • The decision given by judges shall be final and binding. Participants who have duly submitted the solution will be presented with a possible reward.

Important Notes

  • No changes will be allowed after submission.
  • Organizers reserve the right to change any rules as they deem fit without prior intimation.
