GENERAL SERIES

Ultimate Detectives

Solve the mystery behind the Mumbai explosions and uncover the truth


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Embark into a thrilling adventure as you delve into the heart of mystery and intrigue. A series of explosions has shaken Mumbai leaving the people in fear! The attackers have left behind cryptic messages and puzzling clues.

What You'll Need:

  • A great team (3 members)
  • Attention to minute details
  • Analytical mind and good critical thinking
  • Problem solving skills
  • Most importantly, PATIENCE!

Important Note: Participation is required in teams of 3. Register with your team during the first round and retain the same team for all three rounds.

Rules & Rounds

Round 1

Solve the clues, and put your skills to use.

  • Rules will be specified by Event Heads before Round 1 begins.
  • Teams must work together to crack initial puzzles and advance.

Round 2

Solve the mystery hidden and buckle up for the next.

  • Rules will be specified by Event Heads before Round 2 begins.
  • Use of vehicles/personal transport is allowed.
  • Progress deeper into the investigation.

Round 3: The Final Hunt

Complete the final hunt across Mumbai.

  • Area of Hunt: Mumbai
  • Rules will be specified by Event Heads before Round 3 begins.
  • Use of vehicles/personal transport is allowed.
  • Race against time to solve the final mystery.

General Rules

  • A team will consist of 3 members only. Team members cannot be changed after registration.
  • UID number is compulsory for participation.
  • Use of vehicles/personal transport is allowed in Round 2 and Round 3.
  • No prior knowledge is required to participate in the event.
  • Tasks at a particular spot should be completed without any fuss.
  • Overall score shall consist of race timings and accurately solving the clues.
  • No. of teams that will progress to next rounds will be decided by Event Heads.
  • Follow safety rules while running.
  • Any case of malpractice found at the time of event by any team would lead to direct elimination.
  • No bullying of coordinators or volunteers will be tolerated.
  • Organizers are not responsible for any damage to personal property or accidents.
  • The decisions of the Event Heads is final in case of any disputes.
  • Failure to comply with any of the above rules shall result in disqualification from the event.
