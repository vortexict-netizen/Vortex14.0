UpBiz

Entrepreneurship and business strategy competition in collaboration with E-Cell ICT


Back to Events

Prize Pool & Info

  • Prize Pool: ₹25,000
  • Offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus. Dates TBA. Exact details provided post-registration.

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: UpBiz offers a prime opportunity to test and refine your business and management skills. In collaboration with E-Cell ICT, this event combines virtual stock market simulation with business pitching to help you turn innovative ideas into reality.

What You'll Learn:

  • Make smart investments
  • Effectively manage finances
  • Turn creative ideas into tangible ventures
  • Gain mentorship from seasoned industry experts

Eligibility: Participants may compete individually or in teams of up to 2 members.

Rules & Rounds

Round 1 Part A: Virtual Stock Market Simulation (Online)

Participants will engage in a simulated stock market trading game using virtual currency.

  • Trading Rules: Only the phone number given by the team leader is allowed to trade.
  • For teams of 2: Only one member is allowed to engage in trading. This decision must be made at registration, and the selected participant will manage the trading.
  • Trading Window: Whole day trading window - participants have the entire day to manage and maximize their virtual portfolio.
  • Teams will invest in various assets: shares, mutual funds, and fixed deposits to maximize returns within the timeframe.

Round 1 Part B: Business Idea Pitching (In Person)

Each team will pitch their business idea to the panel of judges.

  • Pitching Time: 5 minutes per group.
  • Presentation Must Cover:
    • Business concept and its uniqueness
    • Market opportunity and target customers
    • Estimated costs and financial projections
    • Potential impacts, both positive and negative
  • Judging Criteria: Ideas evaluated based on originality, feasibility, and scalability.
  • Shortlisting: Based on combined scores from Part A (Virtual Stock Market) and Part B (Business Pitch), teams will be shortlisted for the final round.

Round 2: Final Pitch

Qualified teams present their entire business strategy to the panel of judges.

  • Presentation Time: 10 minutes per team.
  • Presentation Must Include:
    • All details from initial business concept to final implementation plan
    • Financial strategy and operational details
    • Detailed marketing approach (if applicable)
  • Panel Discussion: 10-minute Q&A session following presentation where judges will ask questions and participants respond to clarify any aspects of their strategy.
  • Evaluation Focus:
    • Clarity, feasibility, originality, and thoroughness of business strategy
    • Participants' ability to handle questions and engage in constructive discussion

General Guidelines & Key Points

General Guidelines:

  • Eligibility: Participants may compete individually or in teams of up to 2 members.
  • Time Adherence: Strict adherence to time limits is mandatory. Any deviations will not be entertained.
  • Conduct: Use of inappropriate language, content, or behaviour will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Late Submissions: Late entries or submissions will not be considered under any circumstances.
  • Final Decisions: All decisions made by judges and organizers are final and binding.

Key Points:

  • Pitch Duration: 5 minutes for Round 1, 10 minutes for Round 2. Exceeding time limit results in point deduction.
  • Team Participation: If participating as a team, both members must be present during the pitch. Teams must decide beforehand whether one or both members will pitch.
