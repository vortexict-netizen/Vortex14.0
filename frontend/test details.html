PRODIGY SERIES

Sr. IDP (Multi Statements)

Multiple problem statements registration for Senior IDP event.

Back to Events

Submission Timeline

  • Abstract Submission Deadline:
    12 January 2026
  • Final Presentation:
    20 January 2026
  • The date, time, and location will be informed once you register. All offline rounds will be held at ICT Mumbai campus during 8th to 12th January.

Prize Pool & Fee

  • Prize: ₹50,000 (per statement)
  • One Problem Statement: ₹800
  • Multiple (2-3) Statements: ₹1,600

Format & Guidelines

Event Overview: Industry-defined problem (IDP) is one of the most anticipated events of Vortex. This event requires participants to come up with innovative and multidisciplinary solutions for industry problems provided by the leaders of the industry.

Key Features: Participants are exposed to pressing issues in the industry. This gives an excellent opportunity to broaden horizons, apply learned knowledge, and find innovative solutions through collaborations.

Eligibility: 3rd and 4th year undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate students with their respective college IDs are eligible.

Team Format: Only teams allowed. Teams should consist of up to 3 members, all of whom have registered in Vortex. Team members can be of the same or different institution, and same or different courses (UG, PG, PhD). All members are required to have college IDs.

Rules & Rounds

Registration & Problem Statement Allotment

  • Registration form should be filled out along with payment before filling out the preference form.
  • Teams will fill out a preference form mentioning their preference for problem statements. In case it is filled multiple times, the first entry will be considered.
  • Teams will be allotted problem statements on a first-come-first-serve basis (based on time and date of payment).
  • A PDF document of allotments will be shared to the email ID of the team leader.
  • A team ID will be allotted to every team after registration. A team can apply for 2 problem statements at max.

Multiple Problem Statements

  • Sr. IDP allows the submission of more than one problem statement.
  • The team has to fill out only one form on the Vortex website to register for multiple problem statements, and a single preference form.
  • Presentations for multiple problem statements are held on the same day. Slots are arranged such that they do not overlap.
  • Late applications will not be entertained for multiple problem statements.

Abstract, Summary, and Presentation

  • Abstract: Submit before the deadline (4th January 2026). It should contain your understanding of the problem statement within 200-400 words.
  • PowerPoint Presentation: 10 minutes to present before judges, followed by a 5-minute Q&A session. The audience will not be allowed to ask questions.
  • Project Report: Must support the presentation and contain all references, assumptions, detailed solutions, calculations, and appendices.
  • Executive Summary: A 2-page summary of the solution is required for judges (Font: Times New Roman, Size: 12, Double spaced).
  • Submission: Participants must email a copy of the PPT, project report, and executive report to vortex.sridp@gmail.com with the subject: "Team ID- IDP(alphabet allotted)". No changes allowed post-submission.

Presentation Rules and Results

  • Not adhering to time constraints will result in negative marking.
  • To maintain transparency, candidates are requested to refrain from using their institution name or logo during the presentation unless asked by judges. Failure to abide may lead to disqualification.
  • The decision given by judges shall be final and binding.
