Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in Vortex 14.0?

Students from any recognized college or university can participate. Some events may have specific prerequisites outlined in their info sections.

How do the Series discounts work?

If you add 5+ eligible events, you get 50% off. Completing 1 full series grants 60% off, 2 series grants 62%, and all 3 series gives a massive 70% discount. Note: Sr. IDP is excluded from discounts.

Where is the fest located?

The fest is hosted at the ICT Mumbai campus, Matunga, Mumbai.

The Event

Vortex is a curated series of competitions, labs, and talks that blend fundamentals with real-world practice. Each track—Prodigy, General, and Pharma—delivers hands-on challenges, live demos, and mentorship.

  • Live problem-solving labs and guided walkthroughs
  • Industry panels on process design, safety, and bioprocessing
  • Career-aligned workshops with certificates and feedback

Institute of Chemical Technology, Mumbai

The Institute of Chemical Technology (ICT), Mumbai, is a premier institute renowned for excellence in chemical engineering, chemical sciences, and allied fields. Established in 1933 as UDCT and granted Deemed-to-be University status in 2008, ICT combines strong academic foundations with deep industry integration.

Known for rigorous education, impactful research, and a distinguished alumni network, ICT Mumbai continues to contribute significantly to industry, innovation, and society.
